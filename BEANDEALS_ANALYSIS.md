# BeanDeals.com - Competitive Analysis

## Executive Summary

BeanDeals.com is a free, community-driven coffee deal aggregation platform tracking 1,500+ specialty roasters with **no affiliate revenue**. This creates both their strength (trust/credibility) and weakness (sustainability questions).

---

## ✅ What They Do Well

### 1. Sophisticated Curation
- **12-factor algorithm** with letter grades (A, A-, B+, B)
- Only posts deals scoring B- or higher
- "Simple 10% off rarely makes the cut"
- Human verification of every deal

### 2. The Deal Grinder (UNIQUE FEATURE)
- Shows example cart optimized for:
  - Free shipping thresholds
  - Discount stacking
  - Maximum value
- Goes beyond just showing promo codes

### 3. Quality Standards
- Specialty roasters only
- Daily monitoring (BeanBot)
- Removes expired deals immediately
- 1,500+ roasters tracked

### 4. Trust & Transparency
- Zero affiliate revenue
- No conflict of interest
- "By coffee lovers, for coffee lovers"
- Boulder, CO passion project

---

## ❌ Their Weaknesses = Our Opportunities

### Major Gaps:

1. **No Email Capture** ⚠️
   - Can't re-engage users
   - Missing primary retention mechanism
   - Relies on users remembering to visit

2. **No Search or Filters**
   - Can't search by roaster
   - No filters by discount %, region, roast type
   - Poor discoverability

3. **Zero User Engagement**
   - No comments/ratings
   - No user accounts
   - No personalization
   - No saved favorites
   - No deal alerts

4. **No Mobile Optimization** (unclear)
   - No mobile app
   - Responsive design unconfirmed

5. **Limited Deal Context**
   - No roaster profiles
   - No coffee descriptions
   - No origin information
   - No brewing recommendations

6. **Sustainability Concerns**
   - No revenue = questionable longevity
   - Passion projects often fade
   - Could disappear suddenly

7. **No Content Beyond Deals**
   - No blog
   - No educational content
   - No roaster interviews
   - Single-purpose site

8. **Aggressive Bot Protection**
   - May hurt SEO
   - Limits organic discovery
