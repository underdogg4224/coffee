# BeanzNeez Freebie & Giveaway Strategy

## 🎁 Why Freebies Matter

**Psychology:**
- Creates reciprocity (they got something free, they'll come back)
- Generates word-of-mouth marketing
- Email list growth (entry requirement)
- Social proof (winners share excitement)
- Content for social media

**ROI:**
- $100 in freebies → 500+ email signups → $500-1,000 in affiliate revenue
- Viral coefficient: 1 winner tells 10 friends
- User-generated content worth $$$ in marketing

2. Offer: "Sponsor a giveaway, get featured"

- [ ] Record draw (video for transparency)

- [ ] Ship prize
- [ ] Ask winner for testimonial/photo

- [ ] Analyze metrics (entries, shares, traffic)

## ⚠️ Legal Considerations

### Required Disclosures

**Official Rules Must Include:**
- No purchase necessary to enter
- Void where prohibited
- Eligibility (age, location)
- Entry period dates
- Drawing method
- Prize description and ARV
- Odds of winning
- Winner notification method
- Privacy policy
- Sponsor info

**Templates:**
- Use RafflePress/KingSumo built-in templates
- Or consult lawyer for custom rules
- Post on dedicated /giveaway-rules page

### Tax Implications

**For Winners:**
- Prizes $600+ require 1099 form (US)
- Winner responsible for taxes
- Disclose in rules

**For Us:**
- Track prize costs (tax deductible marketing expense)
- Keep receipts
