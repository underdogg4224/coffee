# Coffee Deals Affiliate Site - Business Plan

## Business Model

**Type:** Deal aggregator and affiliate marketing site
**Model:** Similar to BeanDeals, but monetized from day one

### Revenue Streams
1. **Affiliate Commissions** (Primary)
   - Individual roaster affiliate programs (5-20% commission)
   - Coffee subscription services (recurring commissions)
   - Coffee equipment/accessories (Amazon Associates, specific brands)
   - Affiliate networks (ShareASale, CJ Affiliate, Impact)

2. **Display Advertising** (Secondary)
   - Google AdSense (immediate)
   - Mediavine/AdThrive (requires 50k+ monthly sessions)
   - Direct ad sales (future)

3. **Sponsored Content** (Future)
   - Featured deal placements
   - Roaster spotlights
   - Newsletter sponsorships

4. **Exclusive Deals** (Growth Phase) 🎯
   - Direct partnerships with roasters for site-exclusive discounts
   - Custom promo codes (e.g., "YOURSITE25")
   - Flat fees or higher commission rates for exclusives
   - Time-limited flash sales for your audience only

## Growth Strategy: From Affiliate to Direct Deals

### Phase 1: Affiliate Only (Months 1-6)
**Goal:** Build traffic and prove audience quality
- Post public deals from roasters
- Earn standard affiliate commissions
- Grow email list to 1,000+ subscribers
- Reach 10,000+ monthly visitors

**Metrics to track:**
- Click-through rates on deals
- Conversion rates by roaster
- Average order value
- Email engagement rates

### Phase 2: Hybrid Model (Months 6-12)
**Goal:** Negotiate first exclusive deals

**Pitch to roasters once you have:**
- 25,000+ monthly visitors
- 2,000+ email subscribers
- Proven conversion data from their affiliate links
- Engaged social media following

**Negotiation angles:**
1. **Exclusive discount code**
   - "Give our users 25% off vs. your standard 15%"
   - You get: Higher commissions (20-30%) or flat fee per sale

2. **Flash sale partnership**
   - Limited-time exclusive (24-48 hours)
   - Email blast to your list
   - Revenue share or flat sponsorship fee

3. **Product launch partner**
   - Early access to new coffee releases
   - Exclusive pre-order for your audience
   - Commission + promotion fee

**Revenue uplift:**
- Standard affiliate: $50 order × 15% = $7.50
- Exclusive deal: $50 order × 25% commission = $12.50
- OR flat fee: $500 for featured exclusive + affiliate %

### Phase 3: Premium Partnerships (Year 2+)
**Goal:** Become a must-have marketing channel for roasters

**What you can offer:**
- 100,000+ monthly visitors
- 10,000+ engaged email subscribers
- Social media following
- Proven ROI data

**Premium offerings:**
1. **Annual partnership packages**
   - 12 exclusive deals throughout year
   - Featured roaster profile
   - Dedicated email sends
   - Social media promotion
   - **Price:** $5,000-15,000/year

2. **Subscription box partnership**
   - Curated "Site Picks" monthly box
   - You select beans from partners
   - Revenue share with roasters
   - Build your own brand

3. **Consulting/marketing services**
   - Help roasters optimize their own sites
   - Email marketing for roasters
   - Leverage your coffee audience expertise

## Technical Stack Options

### **Option 1: WordPress (Recommended for MVP)**
- **Pros:** Fast to launch, massive plugin ecosystem, easy deal posting
- **Cons:** Can be slow, requires maintenance
- **Plugins Needed:**
  - ThirstyAffiliates (affiliate link management)
  - WP Coupons Pro (deal posting)
  - Yoast SEO
  - MonsterInsights (Google Analytics)
  - MailChimp integration

### **Option 2: Custom Build (Next.js/React)**
- **Pros:** Full control, better performance, modern UX
- **Cons:** Slower to build, requires dev skills
- **Stack:** Next.js, PostgreSQL, Tailwind CSS, Vercel hosting

### **Option 3: Webflow + Airtable**
- **Pros:** Visual design, no-code, fast iteration
- **Cons:** Limited customization, recurring costs
- **Use case:** Design-first approach

**RECOMMENDATION: Start with WordPress, migrate to custom later**

## Risk Mitigation

### **Risks**
1. ❌ Affiliate program rejections - Apply to many, diversify
2. ❌ Low traffic initially - Focus on SEO, be patient
3. ❌ Deal verification accuracy - Manual review process
4. ❌ Roaster complaints - Clear disclosure, fair policies
5. ❌ Competition from BeanDeals - Differentiate with features

### **Contingencies**
- Build email list from day one (owned audience)
- Create evergreen content (long-term SEO value)
- Diversify affiliate partners (don't rely on one)
- Consider sponsored content early if affiliate slow
