## Affiliate Link Management

### Tools to Use
1. **ThirstyAffiliates** (WordPress plugin) - Link cloaking
2. **Pretty Links** - Alternative to ThirstyAffiliates
3. **Spreadsheet** - Track all affiliate IDs

### Link Structure
- Branded: `yourdomain.com/recommends/trade-coffee`
- Deal-specific: `yourdomain.com/deal/stumptown-25off`

### Tracking
- Use UTM parameters: `?utm_source=blog&utm_medium=review&utm_campaign=trade-coffee`
- Track in Google Analytics
- Monitor affiliate dashboards weekly

## Compliance & Disclosure

### FTC Requirements
⚠️ **MUST disclose affiliate relationships clearly**

**Good disclosure examples:**
- "We may earn a commission if you purchase through our links"
- "As an Amazon Associate, we earn from qualifying purchases"
- Place at top of page, visible without scrolling

**Bad disclosure:**
- Hidden in footer
- Unclear language ("we may be compensated")
- No disclosure at all

### WordPress Implementation
Add disclosure to:
- [ ] Every blog post with affiliate links
- [ ] Deal pages
- [ ] Homepage footer
- [ ] About page
