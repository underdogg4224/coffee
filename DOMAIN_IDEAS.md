# Domain Name Ideas - Coffee Deals Site

## Naming Strategy

### What Makes a Good Domain?
✅ **Memorability** - Easy to recall and type
✅ **Brandability** - Unique, not generic
✅ **SEO-friendly** - Keywords help but not required
✅ **Short** - Under 15 characters ideal
✅ **Clear** - Obviously about coffee/deals
❌ **Avoid hyphens** - Hard to communicate verbally
❌ **Avoid numbers** - Confusing (2 vs two)

## Alternative TLDs (If .com Taken)

Consider these TLDs if .com unavailable:
- **.co** - Clean, professional (e.g., CoffeeDeals.co)
- **.io** - Tech-forward (e.g., BeanDeals.io)
- **.deals** - Descriptive (e.g., Coffee.deals)
- **.club** - Community (e.g., Bean.club)
- **.coffee** - Industry-specific (e.g., Daily.coffee)

⚠️ **Avoid:** .net, .org (confusing), .biz (looks spammy)

## Domain Availability Checklist

Once you pick 3-5 favorites:

1. [ ] Check domain registrar (Namecheap, GoDaddy, Google Domains)
2. [ ] Verify social media handles available (@username on Twitter, Instagram)
3. [ ] Google the name - any conflicts?
4. [ ] Check trademark database (USPTO.gov)
5. [ ] Say it out loud - easy to pronounce?
6. [ ] Test spelling - would people misspell it?

## Branding Considerations

Once you have domain:
- Keep logo simple (text-based fine for MVP)
- Color palette: Browns, creams, oranges (coffee colors)
- Font: Modern sans-serif (clean) or coffee-shop vintage
- Tagline ideas:
  - "Your Daily Coffee Deals"
  - "Fresh Roasted Savings"
  - "Specialty Coffee for Less"
  - "Discover Coffee Deals from 1000+ Roasters"
